1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We may collect the following personal information to provide you with the best experience:

  • Name / Username
  • Age / Date of birth (to ensure 18+ eligibility)
  • Gender
  • Profile photos, videos, or audio
  • Email address or mobile number

Purpose: Account creation and verification, user matching and interaction, customer support.

1.2 User Content

We collect content you create and share on Lumivox:

  • Messages and chats
  • Voice or video interactions
  • Profile details and media shared by you

Purpose: Enable communication between users, improve app experience and recommendations.

Messages are private and not publicly visible.

1.3 Location Information

We collect approximate location data via GPS or IP address to show nearby users and prevent fraud and misuse.

You can disable location access anytime from your device settings.

1.4 Device & Log Information

We automatically collect technical information to improve our services:

  • Device type, OS version
  • App usage data
  • IP address
  • Crash and performance logs

Purpose: Improve performance, fix bugs, prevent suspicious activity.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain Lumivox services
  • Match users based on preferences
  • Enable chat, voice, and video features
  • Detect fraud and abuse
  • Improve app features and user experience
  • Comply with legal requirements

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

3.1 With Other Users

Your profile information is visible to matched users only. You have full control over what information you share.

3.2 With Service Providers

We may share limited data with trusted third parties for:

  • Cloud hosting
  • Analytics
  • Payment processing

These partners are contractually required to protect your data and use it only for the services they provide to us.

3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law or government authorities, such as in response to a court order or legal process.

We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Payments & Premium Features

Lumivox may offer paid subscriptions or in-app purchases to enhance your experience.

  • Payment details are processed by secure third-party gateways (e.g., Apple App Store, Google Play Store)
  • We do not store your card or banking information on our servers

All purchases are non-refundable, unless required by applicable law.

5. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You have full control over your data:

  • You can delete your account anytime from the app settings
  • Once deleted, all personal data, chats, and media are permanently removed from our active systems

Note: Some data may be retained for legal or security purposes for a limited period as required by law.

6. Children's Privacy

Lumivox is strictly for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect or maintain information from anyone under the age of 18.

Underage accounts will be terminated immediately upon discovery.

7. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Secure servers and encrypted connections (SSL/TLS)
  • Restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular monitoring for security threats and vulnerabilities

While we strive to protect your data, no system is 100% secure. We recommend using strong passwords and being cautious with the information you share.

8. User Responsibilities

To ensure your safety and privacy, we recommend:

  • Do not share sensitive personal or financial information with other users
  • Meetings arranged outside the app are at your own risk
  • Report fake or abusive profiles immediately using our reporting tools
  • Keep your login credentials secure and confidential

9. Cookies & Tracking

We may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Improve app functionality
  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience

You can manage cookie permissions via your device or browser settings.

10. Third-Party Links

Lumivox may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements.

We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email. Continued use of Lumivox means acceptance of the updated policy.

  • Update or correct inaccurate information
