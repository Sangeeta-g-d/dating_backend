Child Sexual Abuse & Exploitation (CSAE) Policy

Lumivox Dating App · Last Updated: 31‑01‑2026

1. Zero‑Tolerance Policy

Lumivox Dating App has zero tolerance for any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). Any content, behavior, or activity involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child is strictly prohibited. Violations will result in immediate and permanent action, including account termination and reporting to relevant authorities.

2. Definition of a Child

A child is defined as any individual under the age of 18, regardless of local age‑of‑consent laws. Lumivox is a strictly adult‑only dating application (18+).

3. Prohibited Content & Behavior

  • Sexual images, videos, audio, or text involving minors
  • Nude, semi‑nude, or sexually suggestive content of minors
  • Sexual conversations, messages, or emojis involving minors
  • Grooming or attempts to build sexual relationships with minors
  • Requests for sexual content involving minors
  • Sharing, storing, linking, or promoting CSAE material
  • Trafficking, exploitation, or solicitation of minors
  • AI‑generated, animated, edited, or fictional sexual content involving minors

4. Age Restriction & Verification

Users must be 18 years or older to use Lumivox Dating App. Accounts suspected of belonging to minors will be immediately suspended, investigated, and permanently removed if confirmed. Age verification may be requested at any time.

5. Reporting CSAE

Users can report CSAE or suspicious activity through:

  • In‑app Report feature
  • Email: lumivoxdatingapp@gmail.com

All CSAE reports are reviewed on priority.

6. Moderation & Detection

Lumivox uses a combination of automated monitoring tools, human moderation, and user reports to detect and remove CSAE content quickly.

7. Enforcement Actions

  • Immediate removal of content
  • Permanent account termination
  • Device or IP blocking
  • Preservation of relevant data
  • Reporting to law enforcement and child‑protection agencies

8. Legal Compliance

Lumivox complies with all applicable child‑protection laws, including:

  • Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 – India
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 – India
  • Other applicable national and international regulations

9. Data Preservation & Cooperation

In CSAE‑related cases, Lumivox may preserve account information, content, and logs and share them only when legally required, cooperating fully with law‑enforcement authorities.

10. User Responsibility

  • Ensure all interactions are with consenting adults
  • Provide accurate age and identity information
  • Report suspected CSAE immediately

11. Commitment to Child Safety

Lumivox is committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, adult‑only dating environment and actively supports efforts to combat online child exploitation.

12. Policy Updates

This CSAE Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of Lumivox Dating App indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
